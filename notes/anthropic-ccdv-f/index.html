How to use this guide

CCDV-F is for developers who build Claude-powered systems. Its exam guide lists eight weighted domains, each made up of weighted skills; these notes will give each skill its own page, explained from Anthropic's documentation with working code. Skill numbers (1.1, 1.2, ...) follow the order in the guide.

About the exam in 2026. CCDV-F has 53 multiple-choice and multiple-response items in 120 minutes. The passing score is 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000, the fee is US$125 per attempt, and the credential is valid for 12 months. It's delivered by Pearson VUE, online or at a test center, and only people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network can register. The current exam guide is version 1.0, effective July 2026. The guide recommends one to five years of software engineering, at least six months with Claude or similar systems, and Python or TypeScript.

Content is changing. Pearson VUE has announced that this exam gets updated content from 7 December 2026, with updated exam guides on 17 November 2026. Domains 2 and 5 are written as samples; the other pages will follow once the new guide is out. The outline below is from version 1.0 and may change.

Architect or developer? Many skills here (agents, tools and MCP, Claude Code, prompting) overlap with the Claude Certified Architect – Foundations notes. The developer exam adds the API mechanics, engineering and operations topics the architect exam leaves out. See Associate, Developer or Architect? for a comparison of all four Claude exams.
