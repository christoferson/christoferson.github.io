ISC2 CC at a glance

Domain 1: Security Principles

Cybersecurity concepts (Objective 1.1)

ClueAnswer
Stop unauthorized disclosureConfidentiality: access control, encryption, classification
Stop unauthorized change or destructionIntegrity: hashing, signatures, change control
Keep systems usable when neededAvailability: backups, redundancy, DoS protection
FIPS 199 low, moderate, high; system takes the highestImpact levels and the high water mark
Claim, prove, permit, recordIdentification, authentication, authorization, accounting (AAA)
Something you know, have, areAuthentication factors; MFA needs different types
SMS or typed one-time codesNot phishing-resistant; FIDO/WebAuthn and smart cards are
Signer cannot deny signingNon-repudiation: asymmetric digital signature (a shared-key MAC does not give it)
Harm from lawful, secure processing of personal dataPrivacy, which is broader than confidentiality
Poisoned training dataAI attack on integrity, sometimes availability

Redundancy, continuity and recovery (Objective 2.2)

ClueAnswer
One component whose failure stops the serviceSingle point of failure
Mirror copies ransomware encryption tooRedundancy is not a backup
Keeps business processes going vs restores IT at another siteBCP vs DRP
Executed first: get people out safelyOccupant emergency plan
Longest acceptable outage; system recovery time; acceptable data lossMTD; RTO (shorter than MTD); RPO
Space and power only; partly equipped; ready to runCold site; warm site; hot site
Changes since last backup of any kind vs since last fullIncremental vs differential backup
Talk through; run alongside; shut primary downTabletop; parallel test; full interruption test
Model slowly loses accuracyModel drift, a continuity risk

ClueAnswer

Domain 3: Identity and Access Management Concepts

ClueAnswer
Joiner, mover, leaverProvision, modify, deprovision
Source that should trigger account creation and removalThe HR system

Logical access controls (Objective 3.2)

ClueAnswer
Only the access the task needsLeast privilege

Entering and approving the same payment blockedSeparation of duties

Data security (Objective 5.1)

ClueAnswer
Decides classification vs carries it outData owner vs data custodian
Top Secret, Secret, ConfidentialUS classified levels (EO 13526)
