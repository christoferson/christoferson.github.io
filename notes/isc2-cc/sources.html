How to use this register

Version or date

The CC exam outline is dated by its effective date, September 1, 2026 (English PDF revised January 2026). NIST publications carry revision numbers and dates; CISA, FEMA and other agency pages are living pages, so the date they were read is what matters.

Last checked

When the source was last read to confirm the notes match it. Objectives 1.1 to 2.1 were researched on November 7, 2026; objectives 2.2 to 5.5 on November 13, 2026.

Used on

The objective pages whose facts depend on the source. Status changes found in these sources are flagged in callouts on the objective pages and summarized on the cheat sheet and the terms page.

Exam outline

ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) exam outline

Version or dateEffective September 1, 2026 (English PDF revised January 2026); replaced the outline effective August 29, 2022.

Facts recorded100 to 125 items in computerized adaptive testing (25 unscored), 2 hours, pass 700 of 1,000. Five domains: Security Principles (24%), Security Governance (17.3%), Identity and Access Management Concepts (20%), Networking and Cloud Security Concepts (21.3%), Security Operations and Incident Response (17.3%); 19 objectives.

Refreshing the guide

When to refresh

  • ISC2 publishes a new CC outline or changes the exam format.
  • A pending item recorded on the pages changes: the CIRCIA final rule, the NIST AI RMF revision, NIST IR 8596 (Cyber AI Profile), SP 800-82 Rev. 4, SP 800-218 Rev. 1, SP 800-92 Rev. 1, CISA's updated NCISS methodology, or the Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act status.
  • A dated change arrives: TLS certificate lifetimes falling to 100 days (March 15, 2027) and 47 days (March 15, 2029), EU Cyber Resilience Act obligations (December 11, 2027), and the post-quantum deadlines (2030 and 2035).
  • Six months have passed since the last check (next: May 2027).

What to re-check first

NIST revisions and withdrawals, which age fastest: SP 800-61, 800-53 releases, 800-63-4, 800-88, 800-70, 800-126 (SCAP), IR 8286 series, IR 8547, CSWP 39 and the AI publications.

CISA directives and goals: BOD 23-01, 25-01, 26-02 and 26-04, the Cybersecurity Performance Goals 2.0, the KEV catalog, the SCuBA baselines and the Zero Trust Maturity Model page.

Frameworks with versions: MITRE ATT&CK (v20 when checked), ATLAS, D3FEND, CVSS, EPSS, the OWASP Top 10 and AI testing guides, and the CVE program and NVD operations.
