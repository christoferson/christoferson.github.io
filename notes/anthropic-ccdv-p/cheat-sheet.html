Numbers worth knowing

  • Exam: 70 items (60 scored) in 120 minutes; pass 720 on 100 to 1,000; US$175; valid 12 months; guide version 1.0 (November 2026).
  • Subagents: spawn depth 3 and 20 at once by default; max_budget_usd caps the whole tree.
  • Compaction and clearing: compaction trigger default 150,000 (minimum 50,000); tool-result clearing default 100,000, keeps 3.
  • Tool search: keep 3-5 tools loaded; up to 10,000 deferred; worth it from about 10 tools or 10,000 tokens of definitions.
  • Rate limits (Build tier, Sonnet 5.5): 10,000 RPM, 5M ITPM, 1M OTPM; cache reads don’t count toward ITPM.
  • Batches: 50% off; 100,000 requests or 256 MB per batch; most within an hour; expire at 24 hours; results 29 days.
  • Caching: reads 0.05x on Opus and Sonnet 5.5, 0.1x on Haiku 5.5; agent-loop median read share 84%.
  • OpenTelemetry (API): 0.75 MB of content per span by default; Usage and Cost API data within about 4 hours.
  • Model lifecycle: at least 60 days’ notice before retirement.
  • Workload identity: token lifetime 60 s to 24 h, default 1 h (wizard suggests 600 s).
  • Scheduled tasks: 100 scheduled runs per hour per account; 30 fires per hour per task.

Traps

  • Agent messages never approve: approvals come from people through the permission system or a recorded decision.
  • Refusals and truncations are 200s: they never reach an error handler and need their own handling and SLIs.
  • Average scores hide segments: gate and monitor per segment.
  • Measure before trusting: judges, AI reviewers and filters are calibrated against people first.
  • Structure beats prompt text: hooks, credentials, allowlists and sandboxes hold; instructions can be talked around.
  • Mitigate first, diagnose second in an incident, after capturing evidence.
