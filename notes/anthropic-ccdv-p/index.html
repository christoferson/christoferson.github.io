How to use this guide

CCDV-P is the senior developer exam, first published in November 2026: it tests operating Claude systems in production, more than building them. Its exam guide lists eight weighted domains, each with a list of skills; these notes will give each skill its own page, explained from Anthropic's documentation with working code. Skill numbers (1.1, 1.2, ...) follow the order in the guide.

About the exam in 2026. CCDV-P has 70 scenario-based multiple-choice and multiple-response items in 120 minutes; 60 are scored, and 10 are unscored items that you can't tell apart from the rest. The passing score is 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000, the fee is US$175 per attempt, the exam is in English, and the credential is valid for 12 months. It's delivered by Pearson VUE, online or at a test center, and registration goes through the Anthropic Partner Academy, as for the other Claude exams. There are no prerequisites; the guide recommends the Developer – Foundations certification as preparation. The exam guide is version 1.0, published in November 2026.

Who this is for. The guide describes senior and staff software engineers, platform and site reliability engineers, and solutions engineers who own Claude systems in production: accountable for uptime and quality objectives, incident response, spend across applications and tenants, and hardening agents against adversarial input, and increasingly directing fleets of coding agents. Candidates work daily with the Messages API, the Agent SDK, Claude Managed Agents, Claude Code and MCP, know how first-party and cloud-platform deployments differ, and have put at least one tool-using agent into production. It builds on the Developer – Foundations notes; see Associate, Developer or Architect? to compare the Claude exams.
