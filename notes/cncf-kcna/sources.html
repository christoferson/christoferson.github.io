How to use this register

Version or date

The KCNA curriculum has no version number; it was revised in November 2025. Kubernetes documentation follows the minor release (v1.37 when checked), and most other pages are living pages, so the date they were read is what matters.

Licence and quoting

Each group says how its text may be used. CC BY 4.0 and Apache-2.0 sources are quoted with attribution in the page footers; sources without an open licence are summarized in our own words with short quotes only.

Refreshing the guide

When to refresh

  • The KCNA curriculum or the Linux Foundation exam pages change (domains, weights, question count, price, validity).
  • A new Kubernetes minor release, about three times a year (v1.38 is due December 16, 2026): feature states, deprecations and removals, and version-specific numbers on every topic page.
  • CNCF project maturity changes, which happen every few weeks (graduations, new incubating projects, archivals), and changes to the TAG structure.
  • Six months have passed since the last check (next: May 2027).

What to re-check first

Feature states and removals: cgroup v1, kube-proxy ipvs mode (disabled by default planned for v1.40), kube-dns, containerd 1.x support, Endpoints API, Service externalIPs, KYAML, pod-level resources, gang scheduling, HPA scale-to-zero, VolumeGroupSnapshot, system component tracing.

Project releases: Gateway API, Helm (Helm 3 security support ends February 10, 2027), the OpenTelemetry Collector (component renames), Prometheus, Jaeger, Argo CD, Flux, and specs such as CSI and OCI.

Community facts: CNCF project counts and recent graduations, TOC and Steering membership, release team roles, KubeCon dates and regions, the CNCF certification list and Kubestronaut rules.
