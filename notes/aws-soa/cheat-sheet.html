The exam

SOA-C03 at a glance
Format
130 minutes, 65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified). Multiple choice and multiple response; no exam labs.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 720. Compensatory: you don't need to pass each domain. No penalty for guessing.
Domains
1 Monitoring, logging, remediation and performance 22% · 2 Reliability and business continuity 22% · 3 Deployment, provisioning and automation 22% · 4 Security and compliance 16% · 5 Networking and content delivery 18%
Candidate
About a year operating workloads on AWS plus a year in an operations role.
Out of scope
Designing architectures, CI/CD pipelines or hybrid networks; developing software; defining security requirements; capacity planning; cost and TCO analysis; billing.

Numbers worth knowing

Compute and storage
gp3
3,000 IOPS and 125 MiB/s baseline; up to 80,000 IOPS, 2,000 MiB/s, 64 TiB
gp2
3 IOPS per GiB, bursts to 3,000 below 1 TiB
io2 Block Express
Up to 256,000 IOPS and 4,000 MiB/s
Elastic Volumes
Up to 4 modifications per rolling 24 hours; can't shrink
S3
3,500 writes and 5,500 reads per second per prefix; max object 50 TB; single PUT 5 GB
Lambda
15-minute timeout; 1,000 concurrent executions per Region by default; async retries 2, events kept up to 6 hours
Auto Scaling
Health check grace period 300 s in the console; lifecycle hook heartbeat 3,600 s by default
Availability and backup
ALB health check
Every 30 s, timeout 5 s, healthy after 5, unhealthy after 2
Deregistration delay
300 s by default
Route 53 health check
30 s (or 10 s fast), failure threshold 3; healthy if more than 18% of checkers agree
RDS
Multi-AZ failover 60 to 120 s; backups retained 0 to 35 days; up to 15 read replicas
Aurora
6 copies in 3 zones; Backtrack up to 72 hours (MySQL); Global Database lag under a second
DynamoDB PITR
1 to 35 days, restores to a new table
Vault Lock compliance
Grace period of at least 72 hours, then immutable
Security and networking
IAM
Role sessions 1 to 12 hours; role chaining 1 hour; password reuse prevention up to 24
KMS
Rotation 90 to 2,560 days (default 365); deletion wait 7 to 30 days (default 30)
ACM
Public certificates valid 198 days; renewal from 45 days before expiry
Secrets Manager
Rotation as often as every 4 hours; deletion recovery window 7 to 30 days
Parameter Store
Standard: 10,000 parameters, 4 KB, free; advanced: 100,000, 8 KB, charged
VPC
CIDR /16 to /28; 5 addresses reserved per subnet; ephemeral ports 1024 to 65535
Security groups / ACLs
60 inbound and 60 outbound rules / 20 rules per direction
NAT gateway
5 to 100 Gbps; 55,000 connections per destination per IP; 350 s idle timeout
Direct Connect
100 prefixes per private or transit VIF by default
CloudFront
Default TTL 24 hours; errors cached 10 s; 1,000 free invalidation paths a month
