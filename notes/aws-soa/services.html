Overview

The exam guide's appendix lists the services and features that questions may involve. The list isn't exhaustive, and a service being listed doesn't mean it gets many questions: CloudWatch, Systems Manager, CloudFormation, EC2, VPC and IAM appear far more than Amazon SES. The exam uses short names (Amazon SNS), and its help feature shows the full names.

For each service, the "Covered in" column links to the task pages where it's explained. The cheat sheet maps question clues to services.

Some services are on neither list: CodeDeploy, CodePipeline, CodeBuild, Elastic Beanstalk, AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery and Direct Connect among them. These notes treat them as background: know what they do and the concepts they implement (deployment strategies, pilot light DR, hybrid connectivity), without depending on their details.
