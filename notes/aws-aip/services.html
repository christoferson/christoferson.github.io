Overview

The exam guide's appendix lists the services and features that questions may involve. The list isn't exhaustive, and a listed service doesn't mean many questions: Amazon Bedrock (models, knowledge bases, guardrails, agents and AgentCore), Lambda, Step Functions, API Gateway, OpenSearch, CloudWatch and IAM appear far more than Amplify or Wavelength. Some features are named only in the skill statements (Strands Agents, MCP, Bedrock Data Automation, guardrails, cross-Region inference, model evaluations, invocation logs); they are listed here under their parent service.

For each service, the "Covered in" column links to the task sections where it's explained. "Background" means the notes mention it only in passing or not at all: know what it does. The cheat sheet maps question clues to services.
