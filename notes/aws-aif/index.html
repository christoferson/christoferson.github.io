AWS Certified AI Practitioner

What AI, machine learning, generative AI and agentic AI are; how foundation models are chosen, prompted, customized and evaluated; how to build AI responsibly; and how to secure and govern AI solutions on AWS. The exam tests understanding of concepts and AWS services, not hands-on building.

How to use this guide

The exam is for people who use AI and ML solutions on AWS, with up to six months of exposure. You aren't expected to build models, write code or design architectures.

  1. Start with Domain 1The vocabulary of AI and ML, the kinds of problems it solves and how models get from data to production.
  2. Then Domains 2 and 3Together they carry over half the exam (52%): generative and agentic AI, and how foundation models are used, prompted, customized and evaluated.
  3. Finish with Domains 4 and 5Responsible AI, security and governance, where many questions ask which AWS service or feature fits.
