Overview

The exam guide's in-scope list is non-exhaustive and may change, but it shows where questions focus. The Machine Learning and Developer Tools groups are tested in depth: expect to choose between them from a scenario. The rest appear as supporting services in security, data and governance questions, so know each one's purpose in a sentence. Statuses were checked against AWS documentation in November 2026.

Read each scenario for the deciding clue: who the user is (business user, developer, data scientist), whether a foundation model or a custom model is needed, and whether the question is about building, securing, governing or paying for AI.

Machine Learning

The AI and ML services at the heart of the exam. Know what each does, and the clue words that point to it.

Amazon Bedrock

Fully managed, serverless access to foundation models from Amazon and other providers through one API, with Knowledge Bases (managed RAG), Guardrails, Prompt Management, Flows, Evaluations, model customization (fine-tuning, reinforcement fine-tuning, distillation, Custom Model Import) and inference options (on-demand, batch, cross-Region, service tiers, Provisioned Throughput). Prompts and outputs aren't used to train the models or shared with model providers.

When it's the answer"Build a generative AI application on foundation models without managing infrastructure", "compare models from different providers", "managed RAG", "guardrails".

Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

A platform to deploy and operate AI agents securely at scale, with any framework and any model. Services: Runtime (serverless, session-isolated hosting), Memory (short- and long-term), Gateway (turns APIs and Lambda functions into MCP tools), Identity (agent identities, inbound and outbound auth, token vault), Code Interpreter, Browser, Observability, Evaluations and Policy (Cedar rules enforced on every tool call). Generally available since October 2025.

When it's the answer"Run agents in production", "agent memory", "connect agents to tools with MCP", "agent needs credentials to act for a user", "enforce rules on agent tool calls".

Amazon Nova

Amazon's own family of foundation models on Amazon Bedrock: understanding models (Micro for text only and lowest cost; Lite, Pro and Premier for multimodal input), creative models (Canvas for images, with invisible watermarking; Reel for video) and Sonic for real-time speech conversations, with a newer Nova 2 generation. AWS offers uncapped IP indemnity for outputs of generally available Nova models.

When it's the answer"Amazon's own foundation models", "cost-effective multimodal model", "generate images or video on Bedrock", "speech-to-speech conversation".

Amazon SageMaker AI

The fully managed platform to build, train and deploy your own ML models: Studio and notebooks, Canvas (no-code ML, including Data Wrangler data preparation), Feature Store, training jobs and built-in algorithms, automatic model tuning, managed MLflow, Model Registry, Model Cards, Pipelines, and real-time, serverless, asynchronous and batch inference. Renamed from Amazon SageMaker on December 3, 2024.

When it's the answer"Train a custom model on our data", "host a model on an endpoint", "MLOps pipeline", "feature store", "document a model (Model Cards)".

StatusModel Monitor, Clarify, Ground Truth and Augmented AI are no longer open to new customers.

Amazon SageMaker JumpStart

A hub of pre-trained, mostly open source models (foundation models and task-specific models), solution templates and example notebooks inside SageMaker AI. Deploy a model to your own SageMaker AI endpoint or fine-tune it in a few clicks.

When it's the answer"Deploy or fine-tune an open source model on infrastructure we control", "pre-trained models with one-click deployment".

Amazon Comprehend

Natural language processing: sentiment, entities, key phrases, language detection, syntax, and detection or redaction of personal data (PII) in text, plus custom classification and custom entity recognition trained on your examples.

When it's the answer"Sentiment of reviews", "extract names and dates from text", "find and redact PII in documents", "classify support tickets into our categories".

StatusTopic modeling, event detection and prompt safety classification are no longer available to new customers.

Amazon Lex

Builds conversational interfaces (chatbots and voice bots) using speech recognition and language understanding: intents, slots and follow-up questions, fulfilled by AWS Lambda, integrated with contact centers such as Amazon Connect, with generative AI assistance.

When it's the answer"Chatbot that books appointments", "voice bot in a contact center", "understand what the caller wants".

Amazon Personalize

Fully managed recommendations: trains models on your user-item interaction data (views, clicks, purchases) to generate personalized item recommendations, similar items, re-ranking and user segments.

When it's the answer"Personalized product recommendations", "customers who viewed this also viewed", "personalized ranking".

Amazon Polly

Text to speech: converts text into lifelike speech in many languages with standard, neural, long-form and generative voices; SSML controls pronunciation, pauses and emphasis.

When it's the answer"Read text aloud", "voice responses", "narration", "accessibility audio".

Amazon Rekognition

Image and video analysis: objects and scenes, text in images, unsafe content moderation, face detection and comparison, celebrity recognition, Custom Labels for your own objects, and Face Liveness.

When it's the answer"Detect objects or unsafe content in images", "compare faces", "verify a live user", "recognize our products in photos".

StatusStreaming video analysis and bulk image analysis have been closed to new customers since April 30, 2026; People Pathing was discontinued on October 31, 2025.

Amazon Textract

Extracts printed and handwritten text, forms (key-value pairs), tables and specific fields from scanned documents, with specialized APIs for invoices and receipts, identity documents and lending documents, and Queries to ask for specific values. It keeps document structure, going beyond plain OCR.

When it's the answer"Extract data from scanned forms, invoices or IDs", "digitize paper documents".

Amazon Transcribe

Automatic speech recognition: converts speech to text in batch or streaming, with speaker identification, timestamps, custom vocabularies, PII redaction, Call Analytics for contact centers and a medical variant.

When it's the answer"Convert call recordings to text", "live captions", "transcribe meetings".

Amazon Translate

Neural machine translation between many languages, real time or batch, with custom terminology and automatic language detection.

When it's the answer"Translate website content or chats into other languages".

AWS Transform

Uses agentic AI to accelerate large transformation projects: modernizing mainframe applications, migrating VMware workloads to Amazon EC2, porting .NET applications and other code modernization.

When it's the answer"Modernize legacy applications or migrate workloads with AI agents".

Developer Tools

Tools for building AI software and agents.

Kiro

An AI-powered development environment (IDE, command-line tool and web) that helps developers go from prototype to production with AI agents. Spec-driven development produces requirements, design and task files before code; steering files hold project conventions; hooks run agent actions on events; MCP connects external tools. Generally available since November 2025; successor to Amazon Q Developer.

When it's the answer"AI-assisted coding from specifications", "agentic IDE", "replacement for Q Developer".

Strands Agents

An open-source SDK (Apache 2.0) for building AI agents in a few lines of code. Model-driven: give the agent a model, a prompt and tools, and the model plans and calls tools in a loop. Supports any Python function as a tool, MCP, multi-agent patterns (agents as tools, swarm, graph, workflow), A2A and OpenTelemetry tracing.

When it's the answer"Open-source SDK to build agents in code", "multi-agent patterns".

Analytics

Services for collecting, preparing, searching and analyzing data, including the data that trains and grounds AI.

Amazon Quick

An AI-powered service for business users to automate tasks, analyze data, build web apps and conduct research through chat with AI agents over company data: Quick Sight (BI dashboards, evolved from Amazon QuickSight), Quick Index, Quick Research, Quick Flows, Quick Automate, spaces and custom agents. Launched as Amazon Quick Suite in October 2025; the successor for Amazon Q Business users.

When it's the answer"Business users chat with company data", "BI dashboards", "automate business workflows with agents", "research reports with citations".

Amazon OpenSearch Service

Managed search and analytics, including a vector engine for k-NN similarity search, combined with full-text (hybrid) search, as managed domains or OpenSearch Serverless. A common vector store for Bedrock Knowledge Bases.

When it's the answer"Vector database for RAG at scale", "hybrid keyword and semantic search", "log analytics".

AWS Glue

Serverless data integration: the Glue Data Catalog (central metadata catalog), ETL jobs to extract, transform and load data, crawlers that discover schemas, and Data Quality rules.

When it's the answer"Prepare and transform data for ML", "catalog data sets", "data quality rules".

AWS Glue DataBrew

Visual, no-code data preparation with more than 250 built-in transformations to clean and normalize data.

When it's the answer"Clean data without writing code".

AWS Lake Formation

Builds and secures data lakes on Amazon S3, with fine-grained (database, table, column and row) permissions on cataloged data.

When it's the answer"Fine-grained access control on a data lake".

AWS Data Exchange

Find, subscribe to and use third-party data sets in AWS.

When it's the answer"Buy or license external data for training or analysis".

Amazon EMR

Managed big data processing with Apache Spark, Hive and other frameworks on clusters or serverless.

When it's the answer"Large-scale data processing with Spark".

Amazon Redshift

Cloud data warehouse for SQL analytics at scale, with built-in ML features.

When it's the answer"Data warehouse", "SQL analytics over large structured data".

Database

Databases commonly used by AI applications, several of which store vector embeddings.

Amazon Aurora

MySQL- and PostgreSQL-compatible relational database; Aurora PostgreSQL supports pgvector for vector search and is a Bedrock Knowledge Bases vector store.

When it's the answer"Store vectors alongside relational data with SQL".

Amazon RDS

Managed relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, Oracle, SQL Server, Db2); RDS for PostgreSQL supports pgvector.

When it's the answer"Managed relational database", "pgvector on standard PostgreSQL".

Amazon Neptune

Managed graph database; Neptune Analytics adds vector search over graph data and powers GraphRAG in Bedrock Knowledge Bases.

When it's the answer"Relationships between entities", "knowledge graph", "GraphRAG".

Amazon DocumentDB (with MongoDB compatibility)

Managed JSON document database, with vector search.

When it's the answer"Document database with vector search".

Amazon DynamoDB

Serverless key-value and document database with single-digit millisecond performance at any scale; often stores conversation state and agent session data.

When it's the answer"Low-latency storage of chat history or session state".

Amazon ElastiCache

Managed in-memory caching (Valkey, Redis OSS, Memcached), used for low-latency lookups and caching responses.

When it's the answer"Cache frequent responses", "in-memory speed".

Compute and Containers

Where models and AI code run when you host them yourself.

Amazon EC2

Virtual servers, including GPU instances and instances with AWS Trainium (training) and AWS Inferentia (inference) chips.

When it's the answer"Self-host a model with full control", "GPU or Inferentia instances".

AWS Lambda

Serverless functions that run code on demand; used for application logic around models, Bedrock agent actions and Gateway tools.

When it's the answer"Run code without servers", "action called by an agent".

Amazon ECS and Amazon EKS

Run containers with Amazon Elastic Container Service or Kubernetes (Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service), including self-hosted model servers.

When it's the answer"Run containerized model servers or AI applications".

Security, Identity, and Compliance

Services that protect AI systems and support compliance.

AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)

Users, roles and policies that control who and what can call which AI services and access which data, following least privilege.

When it's the answer"Control who can invoke models", "service role for a knowledge base".

AWS Key Management Service (KMS)

Creates and controls encryption keys; customer managed keys encrypt training data, custom models, knowledge bases and logs.

When it's the answer"Encrypt with our own keys", "control and audit key use".

Amazon Macie

Discovers sensitive data (PII, financial and health data, credentials) in Amazon S3 using ML and pattern matching, and flags unprotected buckets.

When it's the answer"Find personal data in S3 before training or indexing".

AWS Secrets Manager

Stores and rotates secrets such as API keys and database passwords.

When it's the answer"Keep API keys out of application code".

Amazon Inspector

Continuously scans EC2 instances, container images in Amazon ECR and Lambda functions for software vulnerabilities and network exposure.

When it's the answer"Scan for vulnerabilities".

AWS Artifact

Self-service access to AWS compliance reports and certifications (SOC, ISO including ISO/IEC 42001, PCI) and agreements such as a HIPAA BAA.

When it's the answer"Download AWS compliance reports for auditors".

Management and Governance

Services that record, check and improve AI environments.

AWS CloudTrail

Records API activity across AWS: who invoked which model, changed which guardrail, started which training job, and when.

When it's the answer"Audit trail of API calls".

Amazon CloudWatch

Metrics, logs, alarms and dashboards; receives Bedrock metrics and invocation logs, Guardrails metrics and AgentCore observability data.

When it's the answer"Monitor latency, errors and usage", "alarms", "store invocation logs".

AWS Config

Records resource configurations and their history and evaluates them against rules and conformance packs.

When it's the answer"Check resources comply with our rules", "configuration history".

AWS Trusted Advisor

Best-practice checks and recommendations for cost, performance, security, fault tolerance, service quotas and operational excellence.

When it's the answer"Best-practice recommendations".

AWS Well-Architected Tool

Reviews workloads against the Well-Architected Framework and its lenses, including Machine Learning, Generative AI and Responsible AI.

When it's the answer"Review an AI workload against AWS best practices".

Networking, Storage and Cost Management

Supporting services that appear in AI scenarios.

Amazon VPC

Isolated virtual networks for AI workloads, with private subnets and security groups.

When it's the answer"Isolate training and inference in a private network".

AWS PrivateLink (VPC endpoints)

Private connectivity from a VPC to AWS services such as Bedrock and SageMaker AI without using the public internet. (A feature of Amazon VPC.)

When it's the answer"Call Bedrock privately, without internet access".

Amazon CloudFront

Content delivery network that serves web applications and content with low latency worldwide.

When it's the answer"Deliver the AI web app globally".

Amazon S3

Object storage: data lakes, training data, documents for knowledge bases, model artifacts, logs; S3 Vectors adds low-cost vector storage and search.

When it's the answer"Store training data and documents", "data lake".

Amazon S3 Glacier

Low-cost archive storage. The S3 Glacier storage classes (Instant Retrieval, Flexible Retrieval, Deep Archive) are the recommended way to archive data in S3.

When it's the answer"Archive old training data and logs cheaply".

StatusThe original vault-based Amazon S3 Glacier service is no longer accepting new customers; the S3 Glacier storage classes are unaffected.

AWS Budgets and AWS Cost Explorer

Budgets sets cost and usage limits with alerts; Cost Explorer analyzes spending trends by service, for example Bedrock token costs.

When it's the answer"Alert when AI spending exceeds a budget", "analyze AI costs".

Renamed and retired services

Services you will still meet in older study material and practice questions, with their current status. Most are no longer named in the exam guide's in-scope list, but knowing what replaced them helps with scenario questions.

Amazon Bedrock Agents (now Agents Classic)

Renamed Amazon Bedrock Agents Classic; no longer open to new customers from July 30, 2026. Existing agents keep working with no planned end date. Use Amazon Bedrock AgentCore for new agents.

Amazon Q Business

No longer open to new customers (announced June 30, 2026); existing customers get bug fixes and security updates. Use Amazon Quick.

Amazon Q Developer

The Q Developer CLI became the Kiro CLI; the IDE plugins reach end of support on April 30, 2027. Use Kiro.

Amazon QuickSight

Evolved into Amazon Quick; QuickSight continues as Quick Sight, a feature of Quick, with existing APIs working.

Amazon Kendra

In maintenance mode since June 30, 2026 and closed to new customers since July 30, 2026. Use Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases (managed knowledge base).

Amazon SageMaker Model Monitor

No longer open to new customers (announced June 30, 2026). Alternatives: open-source monitoring (MLflow with Evidently), Amazon CloudWatch, Quick Sight dashboards.

Amazon SageMaker Clarify

No longer open to new customers (announced June 30, 2026). Alternatives: bias metrics with open-source libraries, SHAP, the fmeval library, Bedrock Evaluations and Guardrails.

Amazon SageMaker Ground Truth

No longer open to new customers (announced June 30, 2026); existing customers continue as normal.

Amazon Augmented AI (A2I)

No longer open to new customers (announced June 30, 2026); existing customers continue as normal.

Amazon Fraud Detector

No longer open to new customers since November 7, 2025. Alternatives: SageMaker AI and AutoGluon.

Amazon Forecast

No longer available to new customers. Alternative: SageMaker Canvas forecasting.

Amazon Lookout for Vision, Metrics and Equipment

All discontinued (October 31, 2025; October 10, 2025; October 7, 2026).

AWS DeepRacer

Removed from the AWS console on December 15, 2025; continues as an open-source solution deployed in your own account.

Amazon SageMaker (name)

Renamed Amazon SageMaker AI on December 3, 2024; "Amazon SageMaker" now names the unified data, analytics and AI platform.
