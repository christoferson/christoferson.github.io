How to use this register

Version or date

The version or publication date shown on the source when it was used. The exam guide has numbered revisions; AWS service guides are living documents without a version, so for those the check date is what matters.

Last checked

When the source was last read to confirm the guide still matches it.

Used on

The pages that rely on the source, found by searching the pages for the source's topics. When a source changes, review these pages.

  1. Check the exam guide firstOpen its revisions page; a new revision means comparing every task's objectives and the in-scope and out-of-scope lists.
  2. Check living documents and status changesRe-read the topics listed, especially availability notices ("no longer open to new customers", end of support, renames).
  3. Update the pages listed under "Used on"Then update the dates here.
