Often confused pairs

PairThe difference
Precision vs recallPrecision: how many flagged items were right, TP ÷ (TP + FP). Recall: how many real positives were caught, TP ÷ (TP + FN)
ROUGE vs BLEUROUGE measures recall of reference words (summaries); BLEU measures precision of n-grams (translation)
Classification vs clusteringClassification predicts known, labeled categories; clustering finds groups nobody defined
Fine-tuning vs continued pre-trainingFine-tuning uses labeled examples to change behavior; continued pre-training uses unlabeled domain text to add knowledge
RAG vs fine-tuningRAG adds changing knowledge at query time with citations; fine-tuning changes behavior and style through training
Fine-tuning vs distillationFine-tuning adapts a model with your examples; distillation trains a small student from a large teacher for cheaper inference
Temperature vs top PTemperature reshapes all token probabilities; top P cuts the candidates to the most likely set
Serverless vs asynchronous inferenceServerless: small requests, idle periods, cold starts. Asynchronous: large payloads up to 1 GB and up to an hour, queued
Asynchronous vs batch inferenceAsynchronous queues individual requests on an endpoint; batch processes a whole dataset as one job with no endpoint
Transcribe vs PollyTranscribe turns speech into text; Polly turns text into speech
Comprehend vs TextractTextract extracts text, forms and tables from documents; Comprehend analyzes text for sentiment, entities and PII
Bedrock vs SageMaker JumpStartBedrock: models through an API, no infrastructure. JumpStart: deploy or fine-tune open models on your own SageMaker AI endpoints
Kiro vs Amazon QuickKiro builds application code from specs; Amazon Quick serves business users with chat, dashboards, research and agents
MCP vs A2AMCP connects an agent to tools and data; A2A connects agents to each other
AWS Config vs CloudTrailConfig records and checks resource configuration; CloudTrail records who called which API and when
Inspector vs MacieInspector scans instances and containers for software vulnerabilities; Macie finds sensitive data such as PII in S3
Artifact vs Trusted AdvisorArtifact provides AWS's compliance reports and agreements; Trusted Advisor gives best-practice recommendations for your account
Transparency vs explainabilityTransparency: how the model was built and tested. Explainability: why it produced this output
Model Cards vs AI Service CardsModel Cards document your own models; AI Service Cards are AWS's documentation of its AI services
Content filters vs denied topicsContent filters block harmful categories such as hate or violence; denied topics block subjects you define
Contextual grounding vs Automated Reasoning checksGrounding checks whether an answer is supported by the sources; Automated Reasoning checks whether it contradicts a formal policy
Overfitting vs underfittingOverfitting: great on training data, poor on test data (high variance). Underfitting: poor on both (high bias)
