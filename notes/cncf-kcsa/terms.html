Overview

A quick lookup for the vocabulary of the KCSA exam. Meanings are short summaries in our own words or brief quotes from kubernetes.io (© The Kubernetes Authors, CC BY 4.0); the topic pages give the full detail and sources. Project statuses come from the CNCF Landscape and project pages in November 2026 and change often. Kubernetes versions refer to v1.37, the current release.

ReferenceThe parts of a cluster, what an attacker gains from each, and where they listen.

Domain 2 covers each component in depth; the ports come from topic 1.1.

Out of scope

ReferenceWhat the KCSA leaves to other exams.

The KCSA is a multiple-choice exam about concepts: what each control does, which threat it addresses and which tool or framework fits. It doesn't test hands-on configuration, which is the CKS's job, or offensive techniques; Domain 4 in this guide stays at the level of public threat matrices with detection and prevention. Provider-specific services (EKS, AKS, GKE features) appear only as examples of shared responsibility. Exact tool flags and versions on these pages are there to make the ideas concrete, not because the exam asks for them.
