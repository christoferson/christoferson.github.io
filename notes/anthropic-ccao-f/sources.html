When to refresh

Triggers

  • A new guide version in the Skilljar version picker, or a Pearson VUE content-update notice for CCAO-F.
  • Changes to claude.ai features the pages describe: Projects, memory and incognito, Research, connectors, Skills sharing, effort levels.
  • A new Usage Policy version, or changes to the privacy articles on training and retention.
  • New models, or a change to the current models' knowledge cutoff.
  • Definitions published for the AI Fluency sub-parts, which the pages currently mark as my reading.
