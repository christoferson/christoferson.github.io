Models and context

ReferenceChoosing how much model, and managing long work.

TermMeaningCovered in
Model tierFable 5.2 (most capable), Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 (fastest, cheapest)3.2
EffortHow much thinking Claude applies: Low, Medium, High, Extra high, Max; more is slower and uses limits faster3.2
ThinkingClaude plans before answering; always on in current models (lower effort for less)3.2
Context windowHow much a chat can hold; 1M tokens for current models on paid plans3.3
Automatic context managementNear the limit, Claude summarises earlier messages so the chat continues (code execution on)3.3
Knowledge cutoffThe date a model's training data ends; June 2026 for current models4.4
Usage limitsPlan allowance per session and week; longer chats, bigger models, more effort and Research use it faster3.3
Model deprecationRetiring an older model; a newer one may differ in tone and length5.4

ReferenceDomains 1 and 2.

TermMeaningCovered in

Staged promptsA large deliverable split into steps with a check between them1.2

HallucinationContent that looks right but isn't supported: invented figures, sources or quotes2.2
