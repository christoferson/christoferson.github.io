The exam

CCAO-F at a glance
Version
Exam guide version 1.1 for the content that starts on 7 December 2026 (published 17 November 2026). Before 7 December, the current guide's objectives apply.
Format
From 7 December: 70 scenario-based multiple-choice items in 120 minutes, 60 scored and 10 unscored (not identified). Before then: 60 items.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 720.
Cost, delivery, validity
US$99 per attempt; Pearson VUE, online or at a test center; valid for 12 months; Claude Partner Network organizations only; no prerequisites.
Domains
1 Task Definition and Prompting 15% · 2 Output Evaluation and Quality Control 20% · 3 Capability, Model, and Context Selection 15% · 4 Workflow Design and Integration 18% · 5 Configuration and Knowledge Management 15% · 6 Responsible Use, Data Protection, and Governance 17%
Underlying framework
Anthropic's AI Fluency competencies: Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence.

Domain 1: Task Definition and Prompting

Prompt construction (1.1)

ClueAnswer
Output is generic or off-targetAdd context: audience, purpose, what good looks like, constraints (1.1)
Output in the wrong shapeState the format and length explicitly; give an example of the target
A rule Claude keeps ignoringSay what to do instead of what not to do, and give the reason

Task decomposition (1.2)

ClueAnswer
Deliverable too big or mixed for one requestStaged prompts with a check between steps (staged plan)
Several sessions on the same materialRun the stages in a Project (Project workflow)

Prompt diagnosis (1.3)

ClueAnswer
Output wrong in a specific wayName the gap: missing context, ambiguous instruction, absent constraint, or wrong strategy (four gaps)
Chat full of failed attemptsRestart with a corrected prompt rather than iterating (iterate or restart)

Domain 3: Capability, Model, and Context Selection

Capability selection (3.1)

ClueAnswer
One-off questionChat
Recurring work, same files and rulesProject; plus a Skill for a fixed format; plus code for exact figures (matching)
Question needing investigation across sourcesResearch (paid plans; web search on)
Material in Drive, Gmail, SlackConnectors, with the user's own permissions (connectors)

Model and effort (3.2)

ClueAnswer
Simple, repetitive, high-volume, easy to checkFaster, cheaper model or lower effort (faster is right)
Complex, high-stakes, many constraintsMore capable model and higher effort, plus review
"Use Max on everything"Slower, uses limits faster, no gain on simple work

Context and memory (3.3)

ClueAnswer
Long chat cluttered with rejected materialChecked summary, then a new chat (handover)
Ongoing work needing the same documents and decisionsPersist to Project knowledge
Stale or wrong memoryReview, edit or delete topics; deleting a chat doesn't delete its memories (memory quality)
One-off sensitive chatIncognito (not saved to history or memory)

Numbers worth knowing

  • Context window: 1M tokens in chat for the current models on paid plans; near the limit Claude summarises earlier messages (code execution on).
  • Projects: Free accounts up to five; paid plans switch to retrieval as knowledge grows (up to about 10 times the capacity).
  • Files: up to 30 MB for code execution and file creation; Gmail attachments up to about 12 MB.
  • Artifacts: made for self-contained content, typically over 15 lines.
  • Connectors: the Help Center suggests loading tools on demand with 10 or more active; Free users get one custom connector.
  • Incognito on Team and Enterprise: still in exports, kept at least 30 days.
  • Deleted consumer chats: removed from Anthropic's back-end within 30 days; feedback kept 5 years.
  • Audit logs: Enterprise only, last 180 days, IDs not content.
  • Knowledge cutoff: June 2026 for Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5.
  • Usage Policy: 15 Universal Usage Standards; 11 high-risk areas.

Traps

  • The biggest option is not the safest: Research, Max effort or the top model for simple work wastes time and usage.
  • Self-checking isn't verification: asking Claude to confirm its own output isn't independent.
  • Fix the layer that changed: a bigger model or stricter instructions don't repair stale knowledge, a changed Skill or stray memory.
  • Settings don't de-identify: incognito and memory controls don't change what was uploaded.
  • A reminder only fixes a training gap.
  • A disclaimer can't rescue a prohibited use; a high-risk use isn't automatically banned.
