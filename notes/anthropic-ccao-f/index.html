Study notes for the Claude Certified Associate – Foundations

Using Claude well as a productivity tool in everyday business work: writing and refining prompts, checking what Claude produces, choosing the right features and models, setting up Projects and knowledge, working within policy and privacy rules, and fixing results that aren't good enough.

How to use this guide

CCAO-F is for people who use Claude in their day-to-day work, such as operations, marketing, project management, education and communications, rather than for developers. Its exam guide lists seven weighted domains, each with a list of tasks; these notes will give each task its own page, explained from Anthropic's documentation and Help Center with worked business examples. Task numbers (1.1, 1.2, ...) follow the order in the guide.

About the exam in 2026. CCAO-F has 60 multiple-choice and multiple-response items in 120 minutes. The passing score is 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000, the fee is US$99 per attempt, and the credential is valid for 12 months. It's delivered by Pearson VUE, online or at a test center, and only people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network can register. The guide describes candidates as having limited to moderate technical expertise, and says more technical work is escalated to Claude Architects and Developers. The current exam guide is version 1.0, effective July 2026.

Placeholder: content is changing. Pearson VUE has announced that this exam gets updated content from 7 December 2026, with updated exam guides on 17 November 2026. Pages will be written once the new guide is out; the outline below is from version 1.0 and may change.

Which Claude certification? The Associate track is for business users; the Developer and Architect tracks are for people who build with the API and design systems. See Architect or Developer? for how those two compare.

Domains

Domain names, weights and tasks are from the official exam guide.

Domain 1Prompting and Task Execution

14%of the exam
  • 1.1Create effective prompts for business and technical tasks
  • 1.2Apply task decomposition techniques to structure complex requests
  • 1.3Iterate prompts to improve output quality
  • 1.4Adapt prompting strategies based on task type (analysis, research, drafting, brainstorming)

Domain 2Output Evaluation and Validation

21%of the exam
  • 2.1Evaluate Claude-generated outputs for accuracy and completeness
  • 2.2Identify hallucinations, inconsistencies, and biases in responses
  • 2.3Apply fact-checking and validation techniques
  • 2.4Determine when human review or additional verification is required
  • 2.5Edit, adapt, refine, and compare outputs for the intended audience
  • 2.6Organize and curate information and select appropriate output formats (artifacts, inline, structured data)

Domain 3Product and Model Selection

12%of the exam
  • 3.1Select appropriate Claude product features (Projects, research mode, chat, artifacts)
  • 3.2Differentiate between Claude model types (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus)
  • 3.3Align model selection with task requirements (cost, speed, quality)
  • 3.4Understand and manage context limitations and memory considerations (when to restart, summarize, or persist)

Domain 4Workflow Integration and Solution Design

16%of the exam
  • 4.1Apply Claude to analyze requirements and use cases
  • 4.2Leverage Claude for research, planning, and process optimization
  • 4.3Use Claude to support solution design, development, and iteration
  • 4.4Integrate Claude into existing workflows to augment or redesign them
  • 4.5Communicate Claude's value and limitations to stakeholders

Domain 5Configuration and Knowledge Management

12%of the exam
  • 5.1Configure Claude Projects with instructions and knowledge sources
  • 5.2Manage uploaded knowledge and connectors (e.g., Google Drive, Gmail)
  • 5.3Create effective system-level instructions
  • 5.4Inform, maintain, and update Claude configurations, knowledge sources, and instructions

Domain 6Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use

15%of the exam
  • 6.1Identify appropriate and inappropriate use cases
  • 6.2Apply data sensitivity, regulatory, and privacy considerations
  • 6.3Follow organizational AI policies and governance standards
  • 6.4Understand the ethical implications of AI usage

Domain 7Troubleshooting and Optimization

10%of the exam
  • 7.1Identify, diagnose, and resolve issues with underperforming prompts or poor outputs
  • 7.2Adjust approach based on feedback and results
  • 7.3Optimize workflows for efficiency and effectiveness
