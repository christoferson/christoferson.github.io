How to use this guide

CCAO-F is for people who use Claude in their day-to-day work, such as operations, marketing, project management, education and communications, rather than for developers. Its exam guide lists six weighted domains, each with a list of skills; these notes will give each skill its own page, explained from Anthropic's documentation and Help Center with worked business examples. Skill numbers (1.1, 1.2, ...) follow the order in the guide.

About the exam in 2026. From 7 December 2026, CCAO-F has 70 scenario-based multiple-choice items in 120 minutes; 60 are scored, and 10 are unscored items that you can't tell apart from the rest. Until then, the exam has 60 items. The passing score is 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000, the fee is US$99 per attempt, and the credential is valid for 12 months. It's delivered by Pearson VUE, online or at a test center, and only people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network can register. There are no prerequisites. The updated guide describes candidates who use claude.ai every day (chat, Projects, Artifacts, Skills and connectors) and who stay accountable for work they produce with Claude's help. Both guides are now version 1.1: the current guide (November 2026) changed only the registration and support sections, and the guide for 7 December 2026 revises the objectives.

Who this is for. The guide describes the candidate as a professional who uses Claude daily in business work, typically a business analyst, project manager, operations lead, marketing, communications, HR or education professional, consultant or knowledge worker, and who builds Claude Projects. It expects regular hands-on use of Claude, structured problem solving, and an understanding of AI limits such as hallucinations, context limits and data sensitivity. It also expects candidates to apply Anthropic's four AI Fluency competencies (Delegation, Description, Discernment and Diligence) and to work in claude.ai with Projects, Artifacts, Skills, connectors and memory controls. The exam isn't described as easier than the others: it has the same format and pass mark, but tests judgement about output, workflows and responsible use rather than technical knowledge. See Associate, Developer or Architect? for a full comparison of all five Claude exams.
