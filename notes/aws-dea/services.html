Overview

The exam guide's appendix lists the services and features that questions may involve, in fourteen categories (guide version 1.1). The list isn't exhaustive, and a listed service doesn't mean many questions: Glue, Athena, Redshift, S3, Lake Formation, Kinesis, EMR, Lambda, Step Functions, IAM and KMS appear far more than Managed Grafana or Shield. The guide still uses the old name "Amazon Kinesis Data Firehose" and lists some services twice; this page lists each service once, under its current name.

For each service, the "Covered in" column links to the task sections where it's explained. "Background" means the notes mention it only in passing or not at all: know what it does. Some services and tools appear only in the skill statements; they're listed after the categories. The cheat sheet maps question clues to services, and the comparisons page sets similar options side by side.
