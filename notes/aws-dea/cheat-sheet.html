The exam

DEA-C01 at a glance
Version
Exam guide version 1.1 (December 12, 2025), which merged the knowledge and skills lists into one list of skills per task. No DEA-C02 or retirement date has been announced.
Format
65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified); multiple choice and multiple response. 130 minutes.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 720. Scoring is compensatory: you need to pass overall, not each domain.
Cost and languages
150 USD; English, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
Domains
1 Data ingestion and transformation 34% · 2 Data store management 26% · 3 Data operations and support 22% · 4 Data security and governance 18% · 17 tasks
Out of scope
Performing ML training and inference, language-specific syntax, and drawing business conclusions from data.

Numbers worth knowing

Streams and ingestion
Kinesis shard
Writes 1 MB or 1,000 records per second; reads 2 MB per second; records up to 10 MiB; PutRecords 500 records or 10 MiB
Kinesis retention
24 hours by default, up to 365 days
Enhanced fan-out
20 consumers per stream; 50 with On-demand Advantage
EventBridge targets
Retried for 24 hours or 185 attempts
EventBridge Scheduler
Flexible windows up to 1,440 minutes; 10 million schedules per Region by default
Processing and orchestration
Glue
Version 5.1 is the default; job timeout default 480 minutes on 5.0+ (maximum 7 days); Flex only on G.1X and G.2X
Step Functions
Payload 256 KiB; Standard history 25,000 events; redrive within 14 days
Lambda
Timeout 900 seconds; memory up to 10,240 MB; payload 6 MB synchronous, 1 MB asynchronous; async retries 0 to 2
Athena
$5 per TB scanned; DML timeout 30 minutes by default; active DML quota per Region (20 in many Regions); CTAS writes at most 100 partitions
Spark defaults
Shuffle partitions 200; broadcast threshold 10 MB; AQE skew at 5× the median and 256 MB
Stores and catalogs
Redshift Serverless
Base capacity 128 RPU by default (4 to 512, up to 1,024 in five Regions); 60-second minimum billing
DynamoDB
Items 400 KB; 3,000 RCU and 1,000 WCU per partition; 5 LSIs, 20 GSIs by default; PITR 1 to 35 days
Glue Data Catalog
10 million partitions per table; 3 partition indexes per table; first million objects and requests free each month
S3
3,500 writes and 5,500 reads per second per prefix; up to 1,000 lifecycle rules; IA minimum 30 days and 128 KB; replication RTC 99.9% within 15 minutes
UNLOAD
Files up to 6.2 GB
Security and logging
KMS
Encrypt up to 4 KB; rotation 90 to 2,560 days (default 365); deletion waiting 7 to 30 days; 10,000 symmetric requests per second by default
Secrets
Secrets Manager 64 KB, rotation as often as every 4 hours; Parameter Store 4 KB standard, 8 KB advanced, 40 TPS shared by default
IAM
Managed policy 6,144 characters; 20 managed policies per role by default; role chaining 1 hour
CloudTrail
Event history 90 days; data events $0.10 per 100,000; digest files hourly
CloudWatch Logs
Retention up to 3,653 days; 100 metric filters and 5 subscription filters per log group; Logs Insights 50 log groups and 60 minutes per query
Lake Formation
100 data filters per principal per table; 50 LF-Tags per resource
Macie
Findings kept 90 days
