The exam

ANS-C01 at a glance
Version and retirement
ANS-C01 is the current and only version. AWS's certification page says the last day to take it is December 31, 2026, and no replacement has been announced. Certifications earned before then stay active for the standard three years; no new ones are issued after retirement.
Format
65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified). 170 minutes. The exam guide lists multiple response (two or more correct out of five or more options) and matching (3–7 prompts, all pairs must be right); the certification page says multiple choice or multiple response.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 750. Scoring is compensatory: you need to pass overall, not each domain.
Cost, languages, delivery
300 USD; English, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese; Pearson VUE test center or online proctored.
Candidate
Five or more years of networking experience, with two or more years of cloud and hybrid networking.
Domains
1 Network Design 30% · 2 Network Implementation 26% · 3 Network Management and Operation 20% · 4 Network Security, Compliance, and Governance 24% · 16 tasks
