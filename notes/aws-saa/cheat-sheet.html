The exam

SAA-C03 at a glance
Version
Exam guide version 1.1, published October 29, 2026 (its only listed change removed Elastic Transcoder from the in-scope list). AWS says guide updates reach the exam about a month after publication. No SAA-C04 or retirement has been announced.
Format
65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified); multiple choice and multiple response. 130 minutes.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 720. Scoring is compensatory: you need to pass overall, not each domain.
Cost, languages, validity
150 USD; ten languages (the Italian exam retires after December 31, 2026); valid for three years. Pearson VUE test center or online.
Candidate
At least one year of hands-on experience designing solutions on AWS, in a solutions architect role, following the Well-Architected Framework.
Domains
1 Design secure architectures 30% · 2 Design resilient architectures 26% · 3 Design high-performing architectures 24% · 4 Design cost-optimized architectures 20% · 14 tasks, 82 skills

Domain 1: Design secure architectures

ClueAnswer

People need AWS accessFederation with temporary credentials, not IAM users; workloads use roles (instance profile, task role, execution role)

ClueAnswer

One event to several independent consumersSNS fan-out to SQS queues, with filter policies; EventBridge for AWS and SaaS events by content
Migrate a JMS or AMQP application with minimal changeAmazon MQ
Multi-step process with retries and human approvalStep Functions Standard (.waitForTaskToken); Express for high-volume short work
Already on Kubernetes / least overhead for containersEKS / ECS on Fargate (containers)

Numbers worth knowing

Security (Domain 1)
Root and IAM
Root MFA required on every account type; 8 MFA devices per user; AssumeRoot sessions 15 minutes; managed policies per role 20 by default (25 max), per user 10; 10 groups per user (Task 1.1)
STS
Role sessions 1 hour by default, up to 12 hours; role chaining caps at 1 hour (Task 1.1)
SCPs and RCPs
SCP up to 10,240 characters, 10 per target; RCP 5,120 characters, 5 per target (Task 1.1)
Security groups and NACLs
60 rules per direction; 5 groups per interface (16 max); rules × groups ≤ 1,000; NACL 20 rules per direction (40 max); ephemeral ports 1024–65535 (Task 1.2)
KMS
Encrypt up to 4 KB; deletion waiting 7–30 days (30 default); rotation 90–2,560 days (365 default); symmetric quota 10,000–100,000 requests per second by Region (Task 1.3)
Shield and WAF
Shield Advanced US$3,000 per month, 1-year term; WAF rate-based windows 60 to 600 seconds (Tasks 1.2, 4.4)
Resilience and performance (Domains 2 and 3)
SQS
Messages up to 1 MiB; retention 4 days default (14 max); visibility timeout 30 seconds default (12 hours max); delay up to 15 minutes; long polling 20 seconds (Task 2.1)
Lambda
15 minutes; 128–10,240 MB; 1,000 concurrent executions per Region by default; payload 6 MB sync, 1 MB async (Tasks 2.1, 3.2)
API Gateway
10,000 requests per second per Region, 5,000 burst; payload 10 MB; REST timeout 29 seconds by default (Task 2.1)
Failover times
RDS Multi-AZ instance 60–120 s; Multi-AZ DB cluster under 35 s; Aurora replica under 60 s; FSx for Windows Multi-AZ under 30 s (Task 2.2)
Availability
99.9% ≈ 8.76 hours per year; 99.99% ≈ 52.6 minutes; DynamoDB global tables SLA 99.999% (Task 2.2)
EBS
gp3 3,000 IOPS and 125 MiB/s included, up to 80,000 IOPS, 2,000 MiB/s, 64 TiB; io2 Block Express 256,000 IOPS, 4,000 MiB/s; st1 500 MiB/s; sc1 250 MiB/s (Task 3.1)
S3
3,500 writes and 5,500 reads per second per prefix; objects up to 50 TB; single PUT 5 GB; multipart parts 5 MiB–5 GiB, 10,000 parts; Replication Time Control 99.9% within 15 minutes (Tasks 1.3, 3.1)
Replicas
Up to 15 RDS read replicas per source (5 recommended for Oracle and SQL Server); 15 Aurora Replicas; Aurora Global Database up to 10 secondary Regions (Tasks 2.2, 3.3)
DynamoDB
1 RCU = one 4 KB strong read (or two eventual) per second; 1 WCU = one 1 KB write; 3,000 RCU and 1,000 WCU per partition; items 400 KB; PITR 1–35 days (Task 3.3)
Kinesis
Shard writes 1 MB or 1,000 records per second, reads 2 MB per second; retention 24 hours to 365 days (Tasks 3.2, 3.5)
Networking
VPC /16 to /28, 5 reserved IPs per subnet; 500 routes per route table; Transit Gateway 5,000 attachments, 100 Gbps per VPC attachment per AZ; peering 125 peers max; VPN tunnel 1.25 Gbps (5 Gbps large tunnels) (Tasks 3.4, 4.4)
