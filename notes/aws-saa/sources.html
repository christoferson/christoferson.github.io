Exam guide

AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) exam guide

Version or dateVersion 1.1, published October 29, 2026 (version 1.0: August 28, 2026). AWS says guide updates are published about one month before they reach the exam. No SAA-C04 or SAA-C03 retirement had been announced when checked; only the Italian-language exam retires, after December 31, 2026.

Facts recorded65 questions (50 scored, 15 unscored), 130 minutes, 150 USD, pass 720 of 1,000, valid 3 years. Four domains: Design Secure Architectures 30%, Design Resilient Architectures 26%, Design High-Performing Architectures 24%, Design Cost-Optimized Architectures 20%, with 14 tasks, 82 skills and 107 knowledge items. Version 1.1 removed Amazon Elastic Transcoder from the in-scope list and five retired services from the out-of-scope list. Guide inconsistencies handled on the pages: AWS Proton (end of support October 7, 2026) is still listed in scope; the in-scope list names services since closed to new customers (Application Discovery Service, Data Pipeline, Snowball Edge, the Glacier vault service).
