How to use this page

Read each list from the top: posts are newest first, and a newer post on the same design usually replaces an older one. A note saying a post predates a feature means it describes an older approach; the design still works, but the current answer may use the newer feature. The posts come mainly from the AWS DevOps and Developer Productivity blog and the Management and Governance blog, with some from the Containers, Database and Architecture blogs, and the dates are publication dates.

The exam guide was written before several service changes, such as CodeCatalyst and CodeGuru closing to new customers and Copilot giving way to ECS Express Mode, so a question may still use older names. The retired and renamed services table on the study guide home lists what has changed.
