The exam

DVA-C03 at a glance
Version
DVA-C03 registration opens December 1, 2026; the last day to take DVA-C02 is November 30, 2026.
Format
65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified); multiple choice and multiple response. 130 minutes, at a Pearson VUE test center or online (the landing page still showed the DVA-C02 values when checked).
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 720. Scoring is compensatory: you need to pass overall, not each domain.
Cost and languages
150 USD; English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Spanish (Latin America).
Domains
1 Development with AWS services 30% · 2 Security 26% · 3 Testing and deployment 22% · 4 Troubleshooting and optimization 22%
Out of scope
Designing architectures and CI/CD pipelines, administering IAM users and servers, selecting foundation models, prompt engineering, RAG design, guardrail design and AI governance.

Numbers worth knowing

Messaging and streams
SQS message
Up to 1 MiB; batches of 10; billed per 64 KB chunk
SQS timings
Visibility default 30 seconds (up to 12 hours); retention default 4 days (up to 14 days); delay up to 15 minutes; long polling up to 20 seconds
SQS FIFO
300 calls per second per action (3,000 messages with batching), more in high-throughput mode; 5-minute deduplication
SNS
256 KiB default, up to 1 MiB on configured topics
EventBridge
Events up to 1 MB; 5 targets per rule; retries for 24 hours or 185 attempts
Kinesis shard
Writes 1 MB or 1,000 records per second; reads 2 MB per second; records up to 10 MiB; retention 24 hours to 365 days
Lambda and API Gateway
Function settings
Memory 128 to 10,240 MB (1,769 MB = 1 vCPU); timeout up to 900 seconds; /tmp 512 to 10,240 MB; environment variables 4 KB
Payloads
6 MB synchronous; 1 MB asynchronous
Packages
50 MB zip for direct upload; 250 MB unzipped with layers; 5 layers; container images 10 GB
Async invocations
2 retries by default (0 to 2); events kept up to 6 hours
Concurrency
1,000 per Region by default; each function adds 1,000 environments every 10 seconds
SQS trigger
Batch size default 10; visibility timeout at least 6 times the function timeout
API Gateway
Integration timeout 29 seconds by default; payload 10 MB; 10,000 requests per second per Region with 5,000 burst; cache TTL 300 seconds by default (up to 3,600)
DynamoDB and S3
Capacity units
1 RCU = one strongly consistent read per second up to 4 KB (two eventually consistent); 1 WCU = one write per second up to 1 KB; transactions cost double
Partition limit
3,000 RCU and 1,000 WCU per partition
Items and calls
Items up to 400 KB; Query and Scan return up to 1 MB per page; BatchGetItem 100 items; BatchWriteItem 25; transactions 100 items
Indexes
Up to 5 LSIs (at creation, 10 GB per partition key value); 20 GSIs by default
Point-in-time recovery
Recovery period 1 to 35 days
S3
Objects up to 50 TB (multipart); single PUT 5 GB; up to 1,000 lifecycle rules; Standard-IA minimum 30 days and 128 KB billed
Security
KMS
Encrypt up to 4 KB; rotation 90 to 2,560 days (default 365); deletion waiting period 7 to 30 days
Cognito tokens
ID and access tokens 5 minutes to 1 day; refresh tokens 60 minutes to 10 years (default 30 days)
Presigned URLs
Up to 7 days with IAM user credentials; limited by temporary credentials' expiry
AssumeRole
Sessions 15 minutes up to the role's maximum (1 to 12 hours, default 1 hour); role chaining 1 hour
Secrets
Secrets Manager values up to 64 KB, rotation as often as every 4 hours; Parameter Store 4 KB standard, 8 KB advanced
ACM
Public certificates up to 198 days (since February 2026), renewed 45 days before expiry
Deployment and monitoring
SAM traffic shifting
Canary10Percent5, 10, 15 or 30Minutes; Linear10PercentEvery1, 2, 3 or 10 Minutes; AllAtOnce
CodeDeploy
Hooks must report within 1 hour; EC2 scripts up to 3,600 seconds
CodePipeline
Manual approval fails after 7 days by default
CloudFormation rollback triggers
Up to 5 alarms; monitoring up to 180 minutes
Metrics
Standard 1-minute resolution; high resolution 1 second; up to 30 dimensions; PutMetricData up to 1,000 metrics per request
X-Ray sampling
First request each second plus 5% by default
