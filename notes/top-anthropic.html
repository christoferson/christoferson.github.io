Certification tracks

Compare architect and developer

Associate

For consultants, sellers and delivery leads who guide customers to the right Claude use cases. CCAO-F.

Developer

For engineers who build with the Claude API, Claude Code and MCP, from first integration to production agents. CCDV-F.

Architect

For people who design Claude solutions end to end: platforms, agent architectures, evaluation, cost and safety. CCAR-F, then CCAR-P.
