How to use this register

Version or date

The CISSP exam outline is dated by its effective date, April 15, 2027 (the 2024 outline applies until April 14, 2027). NIST publications carry revision numbers and dates; CISA and other agency pages are living pages, so the date they were read is what matters.

Last checked

All CISSP research was done on November 13, 2026, from web pages only. Pages without their own facts file (2.1–2.3, 5.1–5.2, 5.5–5.6, 6.1 to 7.7, 7.10 to 8.5) were written from the CC research, whose sources are in the CC register.

Used on

The pages whose facts depend on the source. Status changes found in these sources are flagged on the pages and summarized on the cheat sheet and the terms page.

Refreshing the guide

When to refresh

  • ISC2 publishes a new CISSP outline or changes the exam format, and when the 2027 outline takes effect (April 15, 2027).
  • A pending item recorded on the pages changes: SP 800-57 Part 1 Rev. 6, SP 800-218 Rev. 1, SP 800-82 Rev. 4, SP 800-92 Rev. 1, FIPS 206 and the HQC standard, the BOD 26-04 deadlines, and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework challenges.
  • A dated change arrives: TLS certificate lifetimes falling to 100 days (March 15, 2027) and 47 days (March 15, 2029), EU Cyber Resilience Act obligations (December 11, 2027), and the post-quantum deadlines (2030 and 2035).
  • Six months have passed since the last check (next: May 2027).

What to re-check first

NIST revisions and withdrawals, which age fastest: SP 800-61, 800-53 releases, 800-63-4, 800-88, 800-70, 800-126 (SCAP), IR 8286 series, IR 8547, CSWP 39 and the AI publications.

CISA directives and goals: BOD 23-01, 25-01, 26-02 and 26-04, the Cybersecurity Performance Goals 2.0, the KEV catalog, the SCuBA baselines and the Zero Trust Maturity Model page.

Frameworks with versions: MITRE ATT&CK (v20 when checked), ATLAS, D3FEND, CVSS, EPSS, the OWASP Top 10 and AI testing guides, and the CVE program and NVD operations.
