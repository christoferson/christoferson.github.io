How to use this guide

These notes follow the CISSP exam outline that takes effect on April 15, 2027. It keeps the eight domains and 62 objectives of the 2024 outline but changes the weights, rewords several objectives and adds topics such as AI ethics and AI threats, post-quantum cryptography and operational technology. Related objectives are grouped on one page, explained in our own words with public standards from NIST and CISA; the official outline is on isc2.org.

  1. Start with Domain 1Governance, risk, law, policy and continuity: the management view every other domain answers to.
  2. Then Domains 2 to 5Protecting assets and data, building secure architectures with cryptography, securing networks, and controlling identities and access.
  3. Then Domains 6 to 8Testing and auditing, running security operations and recovery, and building security into software.

About the exam. CISSP uses computerized adaptive testing: 100 to 150 questions in up to three hours, at Pearson VUE test centers; the fee is US$749. Certification needs five years of paid work in at least two of the eight domains (a degree or approved credential can count for one year). Candidates who pass without the experience become an Associate of ISC2 and have six years to gain it. Passing ISC2 CC does not count toward the experience requirement. The outline in force until April 14, 2027 is the 2024 edition.
