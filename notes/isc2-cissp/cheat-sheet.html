The exam

ISC2 CISSP at a glance
Outline
Effective April 15, 2027 (the 2024 outline applies until April 14, 2027); 8 domains, 62 objectives
Format
Computerized adaptive testing: 100–150 items in 3 hours, at Pearson VUE
Passing score
700 out of 1000
Experience
5 years paid work in 2 or more domains (a degree or approved credential can waive 1 year); without it, pass and become an Associate of ISC2 with 6 years to gain it
Weights
D1 13.6%, D2 12%, D3 12.8%, D4 12.8%, D5 13.6%, D6 11.2%, D7 12.8%, D8 11.2%
Mindset
Think as a risk adviser to management: people's safety first, then business goals, policy and due care; choose the answer that addresses the root cause

ClueAnswer

ClueAnswer
