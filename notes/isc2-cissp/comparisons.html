Due care and due diligence

Domain 1: Security Governance and Risk Management. Two duties that questions often swap. Covered in objective 1.3.

DutyMeaningExample
Due careActing as a reasonable organization would to protect assetsDeploying MFA and patching
Due diligenceInvestigating and verifying, on an ongoing basis, that care is being takenAuditing that MFA covers every account; assessing suppliers

Risk responses

Domain 1: Security Governance and Risk Management. What each response does to the risk. Covered in objective 1.9.

ResponseWhat happensExample
AvoidStop the activity that causes the riskDo not store card numbers at all
MitigateReduce likelihood or impact with controlsAdd segmentation and EDR
Transfer or shareMove the financial impact to another partyCyber insurance; accountability stays
AcceptFormally decide to bear the riskDocumented acceptance by the risk owner

Symmetric, asymmetric and elliptic curve

Domain 3: Security Architecture and Engineering. Matching the algorithm family to the job. Covered in objective 3.6.

FamilyStrength and speedUsed for
Symmetric (AES)Fast; one shared key to distributeBulk data encryption
Asymmetric (RSA)Slow; key pairs, large keysKey exchange, signatures
Elliptic curveSame strength with much shorter keysMobile and embedded key exchange and signatures
Post-quantum (ML-KEM, ML-DSA)Resists quantum attack; larger keys or signaturesLong-lived secrets, migration from RSA and ECC
