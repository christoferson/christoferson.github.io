Due care and due diligence

Domain 1: Security Governance and Risk Management. Two duties that questions often swap. Covered in objective 1.3.

DutyMeaningExample

Block cipher modes

Domain 3: Security Architecture and Engineering. How a block cipher is applied to data longer than one block. Covered in objective 3.6.

ModeHow it worksKey point
ECBEach block encrypted on its ownIdentical blocks give identical ciphertext; never use for data
CBCEach block XORed with the previous ciphertext block; IV for the firstErrors affect the next block; needs unpredictable IVs
CFB and OFBFeedback turns the block cipher into a stream cipherOlder stream modes
CTREncrypts a counter to make a keystreamParallel and fast; counter must not repeat
GCMCTR plus authenticationAuthenticated encryption; nonce reuse is catastrophic
