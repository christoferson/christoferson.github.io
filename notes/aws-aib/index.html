AWS Certified AI Business Strategist

Turning AI capabilities into business outcomes: AI literacy for decision makers, AI strategy and value measurement, responsible AI governance, and leading readiness, change and scale. The exam tests strategic decisions, not technical implementation.

How to use this guide

The exam is written for business professionals who work alongside technical teams. No coding or hands-on AWS work is expected.

  1. Start with Domain 1AI literacy underpins every strategy, governance and transformation question.
  2. Then Domains 2, 3 and 4Strategy carries the most weight (28%); governance and transformation build on it.
  3. Review with the reference pagesUse the cheat sheet, comparisons, case studies and templates to rehearse business decisions.
