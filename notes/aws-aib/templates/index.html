Templates

Fill-in templates for the documents an AI business strategist produces, from the first use case brief to the production readiness review. Each shows the fields to capture, with a hint in italics for what goes in each, a field-by-field guide to filling it in, and a completed example. They also make a good revision tool: if you can fill one in for a scenario, you understand that part of the exam.

Strategy and value

Templates for choosing and justifying AI initiatives: proposing a use case, scoring candidates, building the ROI case, defining the KPIs that prove value, and assessing readiness.

Use case brief

At the envision stage, to propose a use case on one page so it can be compared with others.

Use case scoring matrix

To prioritize several candidates consistently. Weights are agreed by the steering committee before scoring.

ROI worksheet

To build the business case, then to check it against measured results after launch.

KPI definition

For every KPI in a business case, so before and after are measured the same way.

Readiness assessment checklist

Before setting AI ambitions, and yearly after that. Score each dimension 1 to 5 with evidence.

Governance and risk

Templates for governing AI: the AI policy, the tool register, the steering committee charter, risk classification, release criteria and vendor evaluation.

AI policy (outline)

As the organization's top-level statement on AI, approved by the steering committee or board.

AI tool policy and register entry

To reduce shadow AI. Publish the register where every employee can find it.

Governance charter (AI steering committee)

To set up the body that owns AI strategy, policy and high-risk approvals.

Risk classification questionnaire

At the idea stage, and again whenever the system changes. The highest-risk answer usually sets the tier.

Release criteria

Agreed before building; the system is released only when they are met.

Vendor evaluation questionnaire

Before buying an AI product or engaging an AI partner.

Delivery and operations

Templates for running AI in production: monitoring plans, incident runbooks, production readiness reviews and escalation criteria.

Monitoring plan entry

For each metric that must be watched in production. A metric without an owner and response isn't a control.

AI incident runbook

Prepared and rehearsed before go-live, for each production AI system.

Production readiness checklist

Before moving a pilot to full production or the next rollout wave.

Escalation criteria

For any assistant or agent: when the AI must hand over to a person.

People and data

Templates for the people and data side: announcing change to staff, describing data products and agreeing data sharing with partners.

Change communication message

To announce an AI change to affected staff, delivered by their own managers, well before go-live.

Data product description

For each key data set shared for AI and analytics, published in the data catalog.

Data sharing agreement (outline)

Before sharing data with a partner or combining data across organizations.
