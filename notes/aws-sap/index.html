AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional

Designing complex AWS solutions for whole organizations: cloud-native, hybrid and network architectures, AI and ML integration, data and storage, identity, encryption, multi-account governance and compliance, cost optimization, migration, disaster recovery and resilience, and operating it all through infrastructure as code, CI/CD, observability and automation.

How to use this guide

The exam is for architects with two or more years of designing and deploying solutions on AWS. Each task page explains every skill in the official exam guide with one running example: the company from the Solutions Architect Associate notes, now at enterprise scale, with dozens of accounts in AWS Organizations, an on-premises data centre to migrate, regulated customer data, strict recovery targets and a generative AI assistant. Pages compare design options, give configuration and code, exam tips, common traps and further reading. Topics taught in depth elsewhere (networking, security, CI/CD, ML) are summarised and linked to those guides rather than repeated.

  1. Start with Domains 1 and 2Half of the exam (25% each): cloud, hybrid, network, data and AI architectures, then identity, encryption, multi-account governance, compliance and responsible AI controls.
  2. Then Domain 5Infrastructure as code, CI/CD and DevSecOps, observability and operational automation (20%).
  3. Finish with Domains 3 and 4Pricing models and cost visibility, then migration, disaster recovery and business continuity (15% each).

About the exam. SAP-C03 replaces SAP-C02: the last day to take SAP-C02 is 16 November 2026 and SAP-C03 starts on 17 November 2026, with no beta period. It has 75 questions (65 scored, 10 unscored), multiple choice and multiple response, in 180 minutes, with a passing score of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000. It costs US$300 and is offered at Pearson VUE test centres or online, in English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Spanish (Latin America). The certification is valid for three years; SAP-C02 certifications stay valid until they expire.

What changed from C02. SAP-C03 reorganizes the exam into five domains along the Well-Architected pillars, replacing C02's four lifecycle domains. New topics include generative and agentic AI (Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, RAG, Guardrails and human oversight), resilience engineering (AWS Fault Injection Service, AWS Resilience Hub, Application Recovery Controller), DevSecOps and observability, data and analytics, and post-quantum cryptography. Newly in scope are services such as Amazon Bedrock, Amazon VPC Lattice, AWS CDK, AWS Cloud WAN, AWS Verified Access, Amazon Security Lake and AWS Transform. IoT services, WorkSpaces and AppStream 2.0, media services, the individual AI services, AWS Batch, App Runner, Service Catalog and Proton are out of scope. AWS Application Migration Service is now AWS Transform MGN.
