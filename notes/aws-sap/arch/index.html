All architectures

Grouped by theme, using the same company and accounts as the task pages. Each page stands alone and links the ANS, SCS and DOP architectures where they go deeper. See also the official blogs and references.

Migration and continuity

  • Data-centre migration: VMware and Oracle to AWS: Portfolio assessment, the 7 Rs and migration waves, AWS Transform MGN for servers, DMS and Schema Conversion for Oracle, cutover and landing-zone readiness.
  • Multi-Region disaster recovery tiers: Backup and restore, pilot light, warm standby and active/active for different workloads, with Aurora Global Database, DynamoDB global tables and ARC.

Design for new solutions

  • Monolith to event-driven microservices: Strangler fig migration of the order system to EventBridge, SQS, Step Functions and containers, with sagas and idempotency.
  • Multi-account generative AI platform: A central Bedrock platform with RAG, agents, guardrails, inference profiles and cost and quota governance for every business unit.
  • Lakehouse data platform: S3 Tables and Apache Iceberg, Lake Formation permissions, Glue, Redshift data sharing and cross-account analytics.

Cost

  • Cost optimization at scale: Savings Plans and Reserved Instances, Spot and Graviton, rightsizing, data transfer and cost allocation with chargeback across the organization.
