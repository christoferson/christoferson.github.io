Exam guide

AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional (SAP-C03) exam guide

Version or dateEffective November 17, 2026; announced September 1, 2026, with registration and the guide from October 27, 2026. SAP-C02 could be taken until November 16, 2026. There was no beta exam.

Facts recorded75 questions (65 scored, 10 unscored), 180 minutes, 300 USD, pass 720 of 1,000, valid 3 years; English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Spanish (Latin America). Five domains: Architecture Design and Implementation Built for the Cloud 25%, Security, Compliance, and Governance 25%, Cost-Optimized Architecture Design 15%, Resilience, Migration, and Business Continuity 15%, Operational Excellence and Automation 20%, with 21 tasks and 115 skills. Guide inconsistencies handled on the pages: it names Application Migration Service by its new name AWS Transform MGN, uses "AWS GuardDuty" for Amazon GuardDuty, and names Application Recovery Controller in a skill but not in the in-scope list; the "technologies and concepts" list it mentions does not exist.
