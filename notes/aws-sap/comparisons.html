Often confused pairs

  • SCP and RCP: Limits what your principals can do versus what anyone can do to your resources.
  • Cognito user pool and identity pool: Signs users in and issues tokens versus trades tokens for AWS credentials.
  • Pod Identity and IRSA: One trust for every cluster with session tags versus a trust per cluster OIDC provider.
  • Drift detection and drift-aware change set: Reports drift versus puts drifted properties back.
  • Savings Plan and Capacity Reservation: A discount with no capacity guarantee versus capacity with no discount.
  • Pilot light and warm standby: Compute not running versus a smaller copy already serving traffic.
  • Aurora switchover and failover: Planned with no data loss versus unplanned with possible data loss.
  • Vault Lock governance and compliance: Removable with permission versus permanent after the grace time.
  • Essentials log class and Logs Intelligent Tiering: Cheaper ingestion versus cheaper storage.
  • Split charge rules and split cost allocation data: Shared cost spread on the cost category page versus container cost in CUR.
  • cdk diff and cdk drift: Code against the deployed template versus the deployed template against live resources.
  • Synthetics and RUM: Scripted checks on a schedule versus real users' sessions.
  • Resilience Hub estimate and measured RTO: Estimated from configuration versus measured in drills and game days.
  • Predictive and target tracking scaling: Scales out ahead of a forecast (never in) versus follows a live metric both ways.
  • Lambda async DLQ and destination: Payload to SQS or SNS versus event and context to more target types.
