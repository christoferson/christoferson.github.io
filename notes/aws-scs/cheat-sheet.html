The exam

SCS-C03 at a glance
Version
SCS-C03 replaced SCS-C02 on December 2, 2025. Exam guide version 1.0 (publication date March 26, 2026). AWS says guide updates are published about a month before they reach the exam.
Format
65 questions: 50 scored and 15 unscored (not identified). Multiple choice, multiple response, ordering (3–5 steps in the right order) and matching (3–7 pairs, all must be right). 170 minutes. Unanswered questions are wrong; there is no penalty for guessing.
Scoring
Scaled 100 to 1,000; pass at 750. Scoring is compensatory: you need to pass overall, not each domain.
Cost, languages, validity
300 USD; English, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Spanish (Latin America), the last three retiring after December 31, 2026; valid for three years. Pearson VUE test center or online.
Candidate
The guide suggests the equivalent of 3–5 years of experience securing cloud solutions; the exam page mentions five years of IT security and two or more years securing AWS workloads.
Domains
1 Detection 16% · 2 Incident Response 14% · 3 Infrastructure Security 18% · 4 Identity and Access Management 20% · 5 Data Protection 18% · 6 Security Foundations and Governance 14% · 16 tasks, 70 skills

Numbers worth knowing

Detection and response (Domains 1 and 2)
GuardDuty
Severity 1–10, attack sequences Critical; findings export to S3 needs a same-Region KMS key (Task 1.1)
CloudTrail
Event history 90 days, management events, one account and Region, one lookup attribute, 50 results per page; organization trail delegated administrators up to 3 (Tasks 1.2, 2.2)
CloudWatch Logs
5 subscription filters per log group, 1 account-level filter per Region; DELIVERY class keeps events 2 days; 10 resource policies per Region (Tasks 1.2, 1.3)
API Gateway logs
Standard execution log events cut at 1 KB; vended log delivery up to 1 MB (Task 1.3)
Systems Manager
State Manager associations run at most every 30 minutes; aws:approve up to 10 approvers (Tasks 1.1, 2.1)
Response
Idle tracked connections stay open up to 5 days after a security group change; Vault Lock compliance mode becomes immutable after a cooling-off period of at least 72 hours; Detective baseline 45 days (Task 2.2)
Infrastructure and identity (Domains 3 and 4)
AWS WAF
1,500 WCU included, 5,000 maximum; core rule set 700 WCU; body inspection 8 KB on ALB, 16–64 KB on CloudFront and API Gateway; rate-based rules from 10 requests, 60–600 second windows, up to 5 custom keys (Task 3.1)
CloudFront
Origin-facing prefix list counts as 55 rules (Task 3.1)
Security groups and NACLs
60 rules per direction, rules × groups max 1,000; NACL 20 rules per direction (40 max); VPC Block Public Access 50 exclusions (Task 3.3)
Connectivity
MACsec on dedicated 10, 100 and 400 Gbps; GWLB uses GENEVE on port 6081; Inspector network reachability every 12 hours (Task 3.3)
Identity Center
Up to 8 MFA devices; portal sessions 8 hours by default; SCIM tokens expire after 1 year (Task 4.1)
STS
Role sessions 1–12 hours; chaining 1 hour; GetSessionToken up to 36 hours (root 1 hour) (Task 4.1)
IAM
Managed policies per role 20 by default (25 max); unused access analyzers report on a chosen window, such as 90 days (Task 4.2)
Data protection and governance (Domains 5 and 6)
KMS
Encrypt up to 4 KB; automatic rotation 90–2,560 days (AWS_KMS origin only); import token 24 hours; imported material expiry up to 365 days; XKS proxy 250 ms and 1,800 requests per second (Tasks 5.2, 5.3)
Certificates
ACM public certificates last 198 days, renewal starts 45 days before expiry; Private CA short-lived certificates up to 7 days (Tasks 5.1, 5.3)
Secrets
Secrets Manager rotation at most every 4 hours; deletion window 7–30 days; RDS-managed master passwords rotate every 7 days; Parameter Store values 4 KB (standard) or 8 KB (advanced) (Task 5.3)
S3 storage
Minimum storage durations 30, 90 and 180 days; Backup cold storage needs at least 90 more days (Task 5.2)
Organizations
SCP 10 per target and 10,240 characters; RCP 5 per target and 5,120 characters; closed accounts linger 90 days (Task 6.1)
Tags
50 per resource; keys 128 and values 256 characters; required-tags checks up to 6 tags (Task 6.2)
Firewall Manager
US$100 per policy per Region per month; 50 policies per organization per Region; NACL account rules 5,000–32,000 (Task 6.2)
AWS Config
1,000 rules per account per Region; 50 conformance packs per account, 130 rules each; up to 3 delegated administrators; remediation default 5 attempts and 60 seconds; continuous CI US$0.003, daily CI US$0.012 (us-east-1) (Task 6.3)
