How to use this guide

The exam is for people with at least a year of building ML solutions with SageMaker AI, Amazon Bedrock and other AWS services, across both traditional ML and generative AI. Each task page explains every skill in the official exam guide with one running example: an online retailer with a tabular churn model and a product-support assistant built with RAG and fine-tuning. Pages give configuration and code, exam tips, common traps and further reading. Deep service detail lives in the SageMaker AI and Amazon Bedrock service notes and is linked rather than repeated.

  1. Start with Domain 1Data preparation is the largest domain (28%): storage and ingestion, feature engineering, Feature Store, data quality and bias, and preparing data for RAG and fine-tuning.
  2. Then Domains 2 and 3Choosing between built-in algorithms, JumpStart, Bedrock and custom models; training, tuning and fine-tuning; evaluating ML and LLM outputs; then endpoints, agents, provisioning and MLOps pipelines.
  3. Finish with Domain 4Monitoring models and agents, cost and performance, and securing data, endpoints and Bedrock access.

About the exam in 2026. MLA-C02 replaced MLA-C01: the last day for MLA-C01 in English was 28 September 2026, and MLA-C02 beta delivery began on 29 September 2026. The beta has 85 questions in 170 minutes for US$75, in English only, with results in about five business days; the exam guide describes the full exam as 50 scored and 15 unscored questions, multiple choice and multiple response, with a passing score of 720. The general availability date, and the final duration and price, haven't been announced. MLA-C01 stays available in Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese until then. The certification is valid for three years.

What changed from C01. MLA-C02 adds about 50 skills on generative AI: vector stores and embeddings, preparing data for RAG and fine-tuning, choosing Bedrock models and RAG patterns, LLM evaluation (BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, LLM-as-a-judge, human review), Bedrock Custom Model Import, agents and their protocols, GPU scaling, prompt management, guardrails, and token and agent costs. It drops loading data into EFS or FSx for training, the old "fine-tune with custom datasets in Bedrock or JumpStart" skill (fine-tuning is now taught more broadly), model pruning and compression, Neo edge optimization, bring your own container, and capacity troubleshooting. Several SageMaker features C01 leaned on, including Clarify, Model Monitor, Debugger, Ground Truth and A2I, closed to new customers on 30 July 2026, and C02 no longer names them; the pages teach the concepts with the current tools. The MLA-C01 notes remain in their own section.
