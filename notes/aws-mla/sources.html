Exam guide

Version or dateNo version number or revisions page published. The exam is in beta: registration opened September 1, 2026, and beta delivery began September 29, 2026 (the certification page shows the beta code ME1-C02). The general availability date is not announced; MLA-C01 remains available until then.

Facts recordedBeta: 85 questions (multiple choice and multiple response), 170 minutes, 75 USD, English only, results within about 5 business days. The guide itself: 50 scored and 15 unscored questions, pass 720 of 1,000. Four domains: Data Preparation for ML and AI 28%, ML Model and Foundation Model Development 24%, Deployment and Orchestration of ML and AI Workflows 24%, Operating, Monitoring, and Securing ML and AI Solutions 24%; 12 tasks and 107 skills. Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese are promised at general availability.
